    Welcome to Watchdog EWS

    Watchdog watches North Texas government activity so you don't have to check it manually. The workflow:

    1. Review newly detected intelligence.
    2. Decide whether to dismiss, monitor, or escalate.
    3. Manage significant issues as Active Matters.
    4. Use Today to see what requires attention.

    You can reopen this anytime, and look up any term, from the Glossary button in the header.
